Metric definitions

  • missed_grant_rate — true ELIGIBLE predicted NOT_ELIGIBLE, k of N_eligible.
  • soft_miss — true CHECK predicted NOT_ELIGIBLE, k of N_check.
  • human_read_share — pairs predicted CHECK, k of N.
  • accuracy — three-way agreement, k of N.
  • automatic_decision_coverage — pairs not sent to CHECK, k of N.
  • automatic_decision_accuracy — correct non-CHECK decisions among automatic decisions, k of N.
  • disqualified_to_eligible_error — true NOT_ELIGIBLE pairs predicted ELIGIBLE, k of N_NOT_ELIGIBLE.
  • negative_to_eligible_error — true NOT_ELIGIBLE or CHECK pairs predicted ELIGIBLE, k of N_negative.
  • quote_validity — proposed disqualifier quotes that are verbatim substrings of the opportunity source_text, k of N_quotes.
  • quote_correctness — NOT_ELIGIBLE predictions whose quote equals the label’s disqualifying_sentence (normalised), k of N_noteligible.
  • latency — median and p95 of latency_ms per row.
